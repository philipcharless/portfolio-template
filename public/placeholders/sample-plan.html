Case study

Agent attraction and onboarding system for a top mega team.

A top-producing real estate team in San Diego County needed a repeatable way to recruit agents and get them producing fast, without recruiting living in someone's inbox.

ClientFAM Realty Group
TypeReal estate · 2023 to present
StackHighLevel, Make, ClickUp

01The problem

Recruiting ran through people's inboxes, and the sales side and the operations side had no shared view of where each new agent stood.

02The plan

Map the candidate journey from first touch to first deal, then automate every hand-off in between.

Candidate lead→ Follow-up→ Booked call→ Onboarded

03What was built

  • Full recruiting system: lead capture, automated follow-up and calendar booking for agent candidates
  • Onboarding tracker that keeps sales and operations on the same live status, with zero manual updates between them
  • Ongoing lead capture and nurture system for the team's buyer and seller pipeline

04Results

Recruiting runs as a repeatable system instead of an inbox, and new agents move from candidate to onboarded with every step tracked.
